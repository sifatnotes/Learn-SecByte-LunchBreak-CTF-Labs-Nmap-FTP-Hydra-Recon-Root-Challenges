The collection brings together LunchBreak and related security challenges covering reconnaissance, service enumeration, FTP investigation, authentication testing, source sleuthing, host discovery, pivoting, and progression toward root access.

## Learning Path

A suggested progression:

1. Begin with **Lunch Break Recon** and **LunchBreak Recon Run**.
2. Continue with **Lunch Break Enumeration** and **LunchBreak Hosts**.
3. Study **Nmap Service Sleuth 5** for service discovery.
4. Explore **PagePeek – Source Sleuthing** for web/source investigation.
5. Continue with **LunchBreak Hydra** for authentication-focused practice.
6. Explore **Lunchbreaker FTP Pivot** and **FTP Footprints to Root**.
7. Finish with **Lunch-Break to Root**, **Venom Flags Forgiveness**, and **Venom Rootchain**.

Adapt the order according to your experience and learning goals.

## How to Use the Labs

1. Select a challenge from the index.
2. Open the direct Learn SecByte URL.
3. Review its objectives, prerequisites, and rules.
4. Perform all activities only within the authorized lab environment.
5. Document your observations, discovered services, and security concepts.
6. Review why each technique works instead of relying only on automated tooling.
7. Continue to related labs to reinforce your skills.
